## Why your first interview feels hard

Most freshers walk into their first interview without knowing what the panel **really** checks. This guide walks you through it, step by step — with *simple* examples.

:::quick-answer
Research the company, practise a 60-second answer to "Tell me about yourself", dress neatly, and send a short thank-you mail the same day.
:::

## Research the company

Spend 30 minutes on the company website and their [LinkedIn page](https://www.linkedin.com/). Note three things:

1. What they sell
2. Who their customers are
3. One recent news item

:::tip
Write your three points on a card and read it in the waiting room.
:::

### Questions to prepare

- Why do you want to work here?
- What do you know about our products?
  and how would you explain them to a friend?

## What to wear

| Role | Safe choice |
|---|---|
| Office / IT | Formal shirt, trousers |
| Field sales | Smart casual |

:::warning
Do not wear brand-new shoes on the day; they hurt by the second round.
:::

> The first impression is made before you say a word.

## FAQ

:::faq
### Can I carry a printed resume?
Yes. Carry two printed copies in a folder.

### What if I don't know an answer?
Say so honestly and explain how you would find out.
:::

:::takeaways
- Research beats memorising answers.
- Neat and comfortable beats expensive.
- Always follow up within 24 hours.
